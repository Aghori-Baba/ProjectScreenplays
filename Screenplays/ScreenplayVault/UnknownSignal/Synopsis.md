Project Unknown Signal

Opening Idea
- A man named Chief is visited by a person called Watchman somewhere in an office in Mumbai. Both standing on opposite ends of a table. Watchman pulls out a zip pouch containing two pens, pulls out one and places it on the top of the desk and asks Chief what colour of the pen does he see? Chief replies Blue and Watchman says that looks like they are on the same page, then he asks Chief to sit down, they have a long day ahead. They both sit. Chief says a Watchman visiting him means something is serious. Watchman says that they have detected an unnaturally strong horizontal Muon burst originating somewhere inside the city of Pune and few days back they detected similar burst originating from somewhere in Varanasi. Chief says that he is aware of the detection in Pune but they couldn't triangulate the source. To which the Watchman says that they believe that Outpost-225 has been activated. The moment he says that they hear strange knocking on the wall behind the Chief. The Chief looks behind him at the wall and says that it is their interrogation room and they have kept a mysterious man detained there who was trying to enter their premises this morning. The Watchman demands to see this person. Chief takes him to the Interrogation room and there is a man zip tied to a chair. The Chief exchanges some words with the stranger who tells him that he is there for a purpose. Watchman asks Chief who is he talking to. Chief says to the man tied to the Chair. To which the Watchman replies that there is no one else in the room. Just 2 of them. Chief is confused. Watchman pulls out the zip pouch containing two pens and pulls out one of them and places it on the table and asks Chief what colour of the pen is it. Chief says it is red. To which Watchman replies that he pulled out the Blue pen and not red, the world around them is not stable. And the moment Chief looks at the chair now it's empty. Sensing danger the Chief draws his weapon and calls for backup. He turns and sees the Watchman pointing towards a corner of the room. And as the Chief looks where he is pointing he sees the stranger standing near the wall in that direction. He points his weapon towards the stranger and tells him to get down on the ground. To which the stranger calmly replies that he is just there to talk about something really important. The Chief asks him how did he break out to which stranger replies that he didn't break out. They just walked into the room together to discuss something important. Chief is confused. Stranger pulls out the zip pouch containing two pens and pulls out one of them and places it on the table and asks Chief what colour of the pen is it. Chief replies what would he like to hear. Suddenly he hears chair move behind him. Chief quickly turns and sees a chair placed behind him and the as he turns to the stranger he sees that he is already seated on a chair. The stranger asks Chief to sit down, they have a long day ahead.

High Concept: An unknown agent known as the Chief is visited by a mysterious visitor who defies the laws of Physics and a girl is being haunted by a strange entity who seems to keep getting closer while hearing strange messages from people she can't seem to see with her eyes.

Opening:

Someone referred to just as the Chief is visited by a person who addresses himself as the Psychic, in a makeshift office like place in a strange looking building. The Psychic claims there is a disturbance in the energy of the room. He mentions that the information around them is unreliable. To prove this he does several tests which greatly confuses the Chief. The Psychic them claims that he can see someone or something through the wall in the adjacent room. The Chief mentions that the adjacent room is their interrogation room and they have kept a mysterious man detained there who was trying to enter their premises this morning. The Psychic demands to see this person.

The Chief takes the Psychic to the interrogation room where the stranger is zip tied to a chair. The Chief exchanges some words with the stranger who tells him that he is there for a purpose. To which the Chief asks for analysis from the Psychic, and the Psychic replies that there is no one in this room apart from the 2 of them. Whatever was there he can't see them anymore. And sure enough the moment the Chief looks at the chair it's empty. Sensing danger the Chief draws his weapon and calls for backup. He turns and sees the Psychic pointing towards a corner of the room. And as the Chief looks where he is pointing he sees the stranger standing near the wall in that direction. 

He points his weapon towards the stranger and tells him to get down on the ground. To which the stranger calmly replies that he is just there to talk about something really important. The gun in the hands of the Chief crumbles and falls on to the ground. The Chief is taken aback and agrees to sit down and talk. The stranger mentions that the information around them is unreliable. Chief mentions that it is exactly what the Psychic said and turns to look at him and strangely there is no one else in the room. The stranger said that there was no Psychic. The Chief was talking to him all along, including in the other room. The Stranger mentions that the Chief just wanted to talk to him in the interrogation room as it had some privacy. He then mentions that there is a mission for him. A very difficult one.

He needs to find a "Gate" that was opened by someone or something and light a beacon near it. The Chief is taken aback as he realizes the stranger is asking him to enter the "Coastal Lattice". The task is extraordinary, but whatever has opened the "Gate" may not bring good to their world. All of this will make sense later he says. But the task won't be easy, whoever has opened the "Gate" is clearly very powerful and will stop him from doing it. So, not to trust the world or the people around him. And then gives him two pens, a red and a blue pen. And tells him when in doubt place specifically the blue pen a surface in front of him. Then Chief says why would Protectorate take orders from COS? To which the stranger replies that the time will tell that the reward was worth the trouble.

Chapter 1:

There is a TV running in a living room and at the corner of the room on the floor Anamika is sitting plopped against a wall. Her posture shows signs of intense depression and deep sorrow. Her phone is ringing and TV is running but everything seems like a blur to her. She is barely noticing anything and ignores the phone first two times it rings. She eventually picks up the phone and places it to her ear without saying anything. A female voice on the other side answers and asks her how she is holding and when did she come back. Anamika replies that she is surviving.

Key Pointers for horror:

- Strange messages in form of Tap
- Strange messages in form of flashing lights
- Ghost coming in and out to provide some context
- Asymmetric story timeline where the events of Chief and Watchman happen at different point in time than Anamika
- Anamika sees Chief inside the Lattice long before Audience knows that he is there
- Door rattles, then flies open and a red chem light is thrown at Anamika
- Chief kicks open a door and throws a chem light which disappears immediately
- Brother is sitting on a sofa and talking to someone on the right in a heavily distorted voice which Anamika cannot see
- Chief enters the room and sees brother sitting on a sofa and starts talking to him
