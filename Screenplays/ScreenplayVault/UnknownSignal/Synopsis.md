Project Unknown Signal

High Concept: An unknown agent known as the Chief is visited by a mysterious visitor who defies the laws of Physics and a girl is being haunted by a strange entity who seems to keep getting closer while hearing strange messages from people she can't seem to see with her eyes.

Opening:

Someone referred to just as the Chief is visited by a person who addresses himself as the Psychic, in a makeshift office like place in a strange looking building. The Psychic claims there is a disturbance in the energy of the room. He mentions that the information around them is unreliable. To prove this he does several tests which greatly confuses the Chief. The Psychic them claims that he can see someone or something through the wall in the adjacent room. The Chief mentions that the adjacent room is their interrogation room and they have kept a mysterious man detained there who was trying to enter their premises this morning. The Psychic demands to see this person. 

The Chief takes the Psychic to the interrogation room where the stranger is zip tied to a chair. The Chief exchanges some words with the stranger who tells him that he is there for a purpose. To which the Chief asks for analysis from the Psychic, and the Psychic replies that there is no one in this room apart from the 2 of them. Whatever was there he can't see them anymore. And sure enough the moment the Chief looks at the chair it's empty. Sensing danger the Chief draws his weapon and calls for backup. He turns and sees the Psychic pointing towards a corner of the room. And as the Chief looks where he is pointing he sees the stranger standing near the wall in that direction. 

He points his weapon towards the stranger and tells him to get down on the ground. To which the stranger calmly replies that he is just there to talk about something really important. The gun in the hands of the Chief crumbles and falls on to the ground. The Chief is taken aback and agrees to sit down and talk. The stranger mentions that the information around them is unreliable. Chief mentions that it is exactly what the Psychic said and turns to look at him and strangely there is no one else in the room. The stranger said that there was no Psychic. The Chief was talking to him all along, including in the other room. The Stranger mentions that the Chief just wanted to talk to him in the interrogation room as it had some privacy. He then mentions that there is a mission for him. A very difficult one. 

He needs to release someone but on a leash. The Chief is taken aback as he realizes the stranger is asking him to perform a "resurrection". The task is extraordinary, but someone very important is in grave danger. All of this will make sense later he says. But the task won't be easy, the higher powers will try to cause disturbance and stop him from doing it. So, not to trust the world or the people around him. And then gives him two pens, a red and a blue pen. And tells him when in doubt place specifically the blue pen a surface in front of him.

Chapter 1:

There is a TV running in a living room and at the corner of the room on the floor Anamika is sitting plopped against a wall. Her posture shows signs of intense depression and deep sorrow. Her phone is ringing and TV is running but everything seems like a blur to her. She is barely noticing anything and ignores the phone first two times it rings. She eventually picks up the phone and places it to her ear without saying anything. A female voice on the other side answers and tells her that she needs to move on and not drown herself with her grief. Anamika asks her what does she want. Callee tells her to come down and she will help her relax.
